Story Retell with Props

Children use puppets, felt pieces, or small toys to retell a familiar story, with teacher prompts about sequence, vocabulary, and characters.

  • PrepareChoose a familiar story and gather suitable props, with alternative-material suggestions.
  • TeachModel retelling, invite children’s ideas, and guide back-and-forth conversation.
  • ConnectUse observation prompts and extend the storytelling idea to families.

One example of the resource format—not evidence of measured child outcomes. Content changes with each activity.

A considered framework connection

TRS-informed teaching and documentation prompts.

Developed with reference to publicly available Texas Rising Star Classroom Assessment Record Forms, these supplemental activities are designed to support selected teacher–child interaction practices, particularly those addressed in Category 2.

Suggested questions, conversational exchanges, and descriptive language give educators practical prompts to review and adapt.

These are supplemental resources, not an official TRS curriculum. The project and activities are not affiliated with or endorsed by the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) or Texas Rising Star. Participation does not guarantee certification, a star rating, or an assessment result. For use outside Texas, alignment with another state’s rating or quality system has not been established.

For child care providers

Interested in receiving activities?

Licensed child care centers in Texas and other states may apply through the standard application. Home-based providers and other caregivers are welcome to contact MMS to discuss possible participation.

No CACFP enrollment with MMS is required. CACFP participation, sponsor choice, and optional TRS status do not affect eligibility or enrollment order. This project’s application is separate from the food-program application on the MMS homepage.

Applications are open. Complete applications are reviewed in the order received; enrollment and continued delivery depend on available funding.

A simple enrollment process.

  1. Apply. Submit a complete application, or contact MMS first if the standard form does not fit your setting.
  2. Application review. Complete applications are reviewed in the order received.
  3. Enrollment or waitlist. Programs are enrolled as funding permits. If funding is unavailable, applicants may be waitlisted and contacted when an opening becomes available.

No child care license number?

Contact support@mealsandmoresupport.org. Our team can discuss your situation and consider possible participation case by case, subject to available funding and delivery arrangements. Contacting us does not guarantee enrollment.

Please contact MMS before using the standard form; do not enter an invented or substitute license number.

Open the application

The current center application asks for the following information. TRS status is optional and for information only; MMS does not verify TRS status.

  • Center name and address
  • License number
  • Licensed capacity
  • Director’s name
  • Email for activity delivery
  • Center telephone
  • Current Texas Rising Star status (optional)

About your information: MMS collects application information to review applications, manage enrollment and the waitlist, and administer the project. For enrolled programs, information needed to arrange delivery, including the license number and delivery contact details, is shared with the contracted activity provider. Enrollment dates and a size tier may also be shared where needed for billing.

Review the notice within the application and the MMS Privacy Policy. For questions or corrections, email support@mealsandmoresupport.org.

Funding, delivery, and usage terms

Activities arrive by email as HTML attachments. Download and open them in a compatible web browser on a smartphone, tablet, or computer. Activities are supplemental resources and do not replace your core curriculum.

Activities are provided at no charge while project funding is available. Continued delivery is not guaranteed. If funding becomes insufficient, MMS will notify participating programs by email before delivery stops. Participants may keep and continue using activities already received under the applicable usage terms.

Activities may be shared with teachers within the participating center for classroom use or printed for use in that center. They may not be resold, republished, or distributed outside the center. Receiving activities does not transfer ownership or copyright. Any case-by-case participation arrangement must establish the applicable delivery and usage terms before enrollment.

Educators are responsible for reviewing each activity for age, ability, safety, and setting suitability before use and for following applicable requirements. These resources are not a validated developmental assessment or a guarantee of a TRS assessment result.

A few useful answers

Questions before you give?

What does a donation support?

Contributions support MMS’s provision of access to supplemental weekday learning activities for participating programs. Availability and continued delivery depend on project funding. See the donation terms for online giving.

Can a donor request an example?

Yes. Email MMS to request a sample activity. No donation is required to request one.

Are activity supplies included?

No. The project provides digital activity resources, including materials lists and suggested alternatives. Physical supplies are not included.

Is this an official Texas Rising Star program?

No. The resources are developed with reference to publicly available TRS guidance, but the project and activities are not affiliated with or endorsed by TWC or Texas Rising Star. They do not guarantee certification, a rating, or an assessment outcome.

Can a business or foundation discuss a larger gift?

Yes. Contact MMS to request a sample and discuss the project’s funding needs. No public recognition or other donor benefit is promised on this page.
