A program of Meals & More Support

Free daily learning activities for licensed child care centers

The Early Learning Enrichment Project provides participating child care centers with classroom-ready learning activities at no charge, funded by private donations to Meals & More Support (MMS).

Applications are open. Centers are enrolled in the order received as funding allows.

At a glance

  • No charge to participating centers while project funding is available
  • Weekday activities by email from our activity provider, ECAB
  • Four age groups: infant, toddler, pre-K, and school-age
  • Texas and other states may apply
  • No CACFP enrollment with MMS required
What centers receive

Classroom-ready activities, every weekday

Activities are delivered each weekday by our activity provider, ECAB (Early Childhood Activity Booster), operated by BizBoost Strategies. They cover four age groups: infant, toddler, pre-K, and school-age.

How activities arrive

Activities arrive by email as HTML attachments. Download them and open them in a compatible web browser on a smartphone, tablet, or computer.

Texas and other states

For Texas centers, activities are designed to support Texas Rising Star quality practices.

For centers outside Texas, activities are general early-learning resources and are not aligned with any other state's rating or quality system.

Who can apply

Licensed centers in Texas and beyond

Licensed child care centers in Texas and other states may apply. Participation does not require CACFP enrollment with MMS, and applying does not affect any center's CACFP participation or sponsor relationship.

How enrollment works

In the order received

We review complete applications in the order received. Centers with a current, verifiable child care license are enrolled as funding allows. If funding is not currently available, your center will be placed on a waitlist and contacted when space opens.

While you participate

No charge while funding is available

Activities are provided at no charge while project funding is available. Continued delivery is not guaranteed. If funding becomes insufficient, MMS will notify participating centers by email before delivery stops. Centers may keep and continue using activities already received, under the usage terms below.

Usage terms

Activities may be shared with teachers within your own center for classroom use or printed for use in your center. They may not be resold, republished, or distributed outside your center. Receiving activities does not transfer ownership or copyright.

Apply

Apply for free activities

This application is for the Enrichment Project only. It is separate from the CACFP application on our homepage.

The application asks for:

  • Center name
  • Center address
  • License number
  • Licensed capacity
  • Director's name
  • Email address for activity delivery
  • Center telephone
  • Current Texas Rising Star status (optional, for information only)

Your information: Meals & More Support collects this information to review your application and administer the Enrichment Project. If your center is enrolled, we share only what is needed for delivery with our activity provider, ECAB. See section 13 of our Privacy Policy. Questions or corrections: support@mealsandmoresupport.org.

Important information

Before you apply

  • Activities are a supplemental resource and do not replace a center's core curriculum.
  • Activity content is developed with AI assistance. Centers should review activities for appropriateness and consult their TRS mentor or specialist as needed.
  • This Enrichment Project and its activities are not endorsed by TWC or the Texas Rising Star program. Participation does not guarantee certification, a rating, or an assessment outcome.
